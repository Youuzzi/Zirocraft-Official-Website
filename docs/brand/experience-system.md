# ZiroCraft Brand & Experience System

## Brand principle

**One brand. Different experiences. One connected ecosystem.**

ZiroCraft is the parent technology studio. Its corporate website communicates the brand, capabilities, projects, and products. Each SaaS product has a separate product experience optimized for daily work. The experiences share a visual foundation without sharing the same layout or interaction model.

## Logo-derived color foundation

The supplied ZiroCraft mark uses a deep navy structure and a cyan-to-blue gradient. The interface palette is derived from those visible colors rather than selecting an unrelated trend palette.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand midnight | `#0B1724` | Primary dark background |
| Brand navy | `#102033` | Secondary dark surface |
| Brand blue | `#176FAE` | Links and supporting emphasis |
| Brand cyan | `#22B8C8` | Primary accent and interactive focus |
| Cyan highlight | `#55CBD6` | Dark-surface accent and hover |
| Cool paper | `#F2F7FA` | Light content surface |
| Cool mist | `#E5F1F5` | Soft panel and product surface |
| Body ink | `#102235` | Text on light surfaces |
| Muted ink | `#465766` | Secondary text on light surfaces |
| Coral signal | `#F07F69` | Restrained status or emphasis accent |

The original logo asset is not recolored to fit a section. Use the supplied light/dark/monochrome variants only where their contrast is appropriate. Never place a dark logo variant on a dark surface or a light variant on a light surface.

## Corporate website personality

The corporate experience is expressive, editorial, and brand-led.

- Strong typography and compact, scannable copy.
- Editorial section compositions and project storytelling.
- Navy foundations balanced with cool light surfaces.
- Cyan/blue accents that echo the logo.
- Restrained coral only for selected emphasis.
- Interactive hero artwork, orbiting geometry, pointer response, hover depth, and subtle transitions.
- Clear navigation to About, Services, Projects, Products, and Contact.

## SaaS product personality

Each SaaS experience is operational, structured, and productivity-led.

- Clear application navigation and information hierarchy.
- Data-dense but readable tables, dashboards, forms, and workflows.
- Neutral surfaces with the same navy/cyan brand foundation.
- Product-specific semantic colors and interaction patterns.
- Subtle motion for state changes, loading, progress, navigation, and data visualization.
- No corporate-style oversized editorial hero inside operational screens.

The SaaS must not copy the corporate website layout. It should feel like a professional application from the same company.

## Motion principles

Corporate motion may be expressive: scroll reveals, kinetic typography, pointer-responsive visuals, hover depth, and ambient movement.

SaaS motion must be functional: short page transitions, clear feedback, loading skeletons, progress, chart transitions, drawers, and form states.

All motion must be purposeful, performant, keyboard-friendly, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Avoid animation that delays access to content or causes layout shifts.

## Accessibility and contrast

- Normal text must meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1).
- Large text must meet at least 3:1.
- Do not use low-opacity text for essential information.
- Cyan is suitable as a highlight on dark navy, not as a background behind low-contrast muted text.
- On light surfaces, use dark navy or muted ink for text.
- Status must be conveyed with text or icons, not color alone.

## Product and project truthfulness

ZiroLogistics and ZiroPOS are internal products in development. Project spotlights must be labelled as internal product development. Do not imply customer deployments, production availability, testimonials, metrics, or released capabilities without evidence.

## Cross-site continuity

When visitors move from `zirocraft.com` to a product website or SaaS application, preserve logo treatment, color tokens, typography principles, focus states, and interaction quality. Allow product-specific navigation, layout, density, and motion to differ.
